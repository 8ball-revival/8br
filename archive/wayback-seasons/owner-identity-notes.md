## Why a bracket names people the group stage does not

The owner's account: players changed their CueVerse ID mid-Season, and the admins updated the
playoff bracket without going back to the groups. So a handle can hold an entry position on a
bracket and be absent from the same Season's participant list, without anybody having entered late.

Their rule of thumb is that the new ID usually belongs to the highest-seeded qualifier who is
otherwise missing from the draw. That is a good place to start looking and not a basis for writing
a record: applied automatically it would silently reassign matches to the wrong person. The step it
feeds is a report — each unmatched bracket handle beside the excluded qualifiers ranked by seed —
for the owner to confirm one at a time.
