# Playoff field reconciliation

Three records of who was in each playoff: the manifest's qualifiers, the archived bracket
page's entry positions, and what the database currently has selected.

A complete page outranks the manifest, because a drawing of the draw is a better record of
who entered it than a roster of who qualified. An incomplete one does not, because an absent
handle then means the capture is short rather than that the player was missing.
