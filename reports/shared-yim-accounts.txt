ACCOUNTS SHARING A YAHOO MESSENGER ID
8 Ball Registry - 8BRCAM archive, 2005-2014
==============================================================================

A YIM was the one identifier the old site captured that a person could not
casually change, so two player records carrying the same one are almost always
ONE person entered twice.

IMPORTANT: the archive extraction already resolved identity BY YIM - each
distinct YIM string became one player record. An exact duplicate therefore
cannot exist in this data by construction, and section 1 below confirms that.

What the extraction could NOT catch is a YIM differing only by punctuation.
king_2068 and king2068 are one Yahoo account typed two ways, but two different
strings, so they became two player records. Section 2 lists those, and they are
the real duplicate accounts in this archive.
